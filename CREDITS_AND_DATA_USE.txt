INTERPRETATION
Ordinary counts use unqualified genus-level entries and deduplicate bees. Tentative and family-only records are shown separately. TX membership and local occurrence do not verify a locally observed association. A genus entry does not establish every constituent species as a host. Original names and family labels can reflect older taxonomy. Missing specialist entries are not proof that a plant has no bee value.

This release does not bundle rarity categories, flight calendars, taxonomic authorities, author prose, photographs, supplementary regional relationships, or cuckoo-bee associations. Botanical metadata is not inherited silently from preceding source rows.
