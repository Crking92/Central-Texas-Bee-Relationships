Sources & methods

What these relationships mean

Relationship source

Adapted factual bee–host associations from Jarrod Fowler (2020), Pollen Specialist Bees of the Central United States. Accessed October 7, 2026. Interface and aggregation: Glass Root Garden.

Texas scope

A bee is included when TX appears in the source States field. Its host links may come from elsewhere in the source region. These are not confirmed Hays County interactions, and genus-level entries do not establish every species as a host.

Complete captured host lists

Version 0.4 restores every captured host genus for the Texas-listed bees rather than keeping only the first. Ordinary plant, garden, and comparison counts include unqualified genus entries and deduplicate bees. Source-listed does not mean every original research record has been independently revalidated.

Uncertainty and rank

Question-marked entries remain tentative and are excluded from ordinary counts. Family-only records do not imply any particular genus and are excluded from genus-based garden totals. Missing plant-family labels remain unstated. Source family labels and older plant names are retained; botanical name reconciliation is further work.

Local observations

iNaturalist supplies optional live sightings only. Those observations do not create or validate pollen-host links. Few or no observations do not establish absence.

Credit and reuse

Source links and access dates accompany the exported relationships. No explicit source reuse license was located, and this dashboard does not grant a license to source material. Author text and photographs are not reproduced. Every future image requires its own creator credit and license check.

Relationships with provenance

One row per bee–host target, including target rank, qualification, source URL, and access date. Tentative and family-only entries remain explicitly labelled in the export.
